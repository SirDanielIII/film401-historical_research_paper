In Mary Poppins, Dick Van Dyke dances with animated penguins. The performance and animation were made separately, then combined using a backdrop lit by yellow sodium-vapour lamps.

Petro Vlahos helped develop that system. He was an engineer who spent decades improving how filmmakers separated actors from their backgrounds. His work also included colour-difference blue-screen photography and, later, Ultimatte electronic compositing. Following his career shows how laboratory engineering became essential to familiar film effects.
